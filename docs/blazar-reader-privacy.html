Privacy Policy

App: Blazar Reader · Package net.blazarforce.blazarreader · Publisher: Blazar Force · Effective November 23, 2026

The short version

  • Blazar Reader does not collect, sell, or share any personal data.
  • No account or sign-in, no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting, no trackers.
  • Your library, reading progress, and settings are stored on your device.
  • The online comic catalog and chapters are downloaded over HTTPS from GitHub.

1. Information we collect

None. Blazar Reader has no user accounts and no servers of its own that receive information about you. The app does not collect your name, email address, contacts, location, device identifiers, advertising ID, photos, or usage statistics, and it contains no advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting software development kits.

2. What stays on your device

To work as a reader, the app keeps the following in its private app storage on your phone or tablet:

  • Comics you import (CBZ, PDF, or folders of page images) and chapters you download from the online library, plus their cover thumbnails.
  • Your shelf list, reading progress (last page read, finished status), and reader settings such as theme, page-turn style, and warmth.
  • A cached copy of the online comic catalog so Discover works offline.

When you import a file or folder, Android asks you to pick it, and the app reads only what you chose. This data never leaves your device through Blazar Reader. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes it.

3. Android backup

Blazar Reader allows Android's standard Auto Backup. If you have turned on device backup in your Android settings, Android may include the app's local data (for example your shelf and reading progress) in your personal device backup, which is handled by Google under your Google account and its privacy terms. Blazar Force cannot access that backup. You can turn backup off at any time in your device's settings.

4. Network connections

The app connects to the internet only to fetch the public comic catalog, covers, and chapter files, which are hosted on GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com and GitHub release downloads). All of these requests use encrypted HTTPS. They contain no account details or personal identifiers from the app. As with any website, GitHub's servers receive standard connection information such as your IP address and a basic app user agent in order to deliver the files; that information is handled under GitHub's Privacy Statement. Blazar Reader does not receive or store it.

Tapping Privacy Policy inside the app opens this page in your web browser.

5. Permissions

The only permission Blazar Reader requests is Internet access, used for the downloads described above. It does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, phone, or device-wide storage.

6. Sharing and selling of data

Because Blazar Reader collects no personal data, there is nothing to sell, rent, trade, or share with third parties, and none is used for advertising or profiling.

7. Children's privacy

Blazar Reader is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help, though the app has no mechanism for sending such information to us.

8. Security

Data stays in Android's private app storage, which other apps cannot read, and all downloads use HTTPS. Comic files are parsed with external XML entities disabled.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If a future version of the app ever begins collecting data, this policy will be updated before that version is released.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy or the app: email canner.gtfo@gmail.com, or open an issue on the app's public repository at github.com/cannergtfo-commits/folio-comic-reader.
